Meet the artist

Hi! I’m Annmarie, professional doodler.

I’m an illustrator, character designer, and game designer interested in the funny, tender, and slightly odd.

That’s me!

My work starts with a pencil, a pile of references, and the question “what if it had a tiny hat?” Edit this paragraph with your own story.

I love collaborating with kind people on picture books, indie games, and identity projects. When I’m not drawing, you’ll find me making ceramics and gardening.

Currently: wonky shapes, tiny marvels, huge flowers.

Have a story to tell?

Let’s make something wonderfully weird.
